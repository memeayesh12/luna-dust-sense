STUDENT SPACECRAFT PROJECT

Luna Dust Sense

Electrostatic Dust Protection System for Lunar Spacecraft

Lunar dust is sharp, electrostatically charged, and abrasive — a real threat to spacecraft surfaces and instruments. Luna Dust Sense models dust motion with MATLAB and vector calculus to predict its direction and time of impact, so electrostatic shielding can be aimed exactly where the spacecraft needs it most.

HOW THE SYSTEM WORKS

From dust trajectory to targeted shielding

The pipeline turns raw dust behavior into a shielding decision, one stage at a time.

  1. 01

    Model dust motion in MATLAB

    Lunar dust trajectories are simulated as vector fields, accounting for charge, velocity, and the spacecraft's local environment.

  2. 02

    Apply vector calculus

    Divergence and directional derivatives of the field reveal where dust is converging and how fast it is approaching.

  3. 03

    Predict direction and impact time

    Each particle's path is projected forward to estimate exactly when and where it will reach the spacecraft's hull.

  4. 04

    Aim electrostatic protection

    Surfaces with the highest predicted exposure are identified so shielding can be concentrated where it is actually needed.
